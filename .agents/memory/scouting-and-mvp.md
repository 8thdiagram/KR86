description: Approved legend labels, honest contribution-based awards, and preservation of historical bonuses

Use position and jersey number to identify legends when no player names were supplied. Do not invent historical player names.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose “Use position and jersey” after being told the roster did not contain player names.

**How to apply:** Reuse the authoritative roster and stable player identity. Matching jersey numbers alone must not merge different people; a genuine multi-role identity remains one person.

Championship MVP selection must come from attributable contributions in that exact Super Cup, among existing legends on the winning team. Do not substitute random selection, ratings, or fabricated individual statistics.

**Why:** The user wants performance-based awards and explicitly requires reporting insufficient CPU simulation data rather than inventing it.

**How to apply:** Preserve historical championships and earned rewards. Report unavailable individual performance honestly for unmeasured historical games or aggregate-only CPU simulations; do not backfill fictional performances. A richer CPU simulation requires its own approved scope if preserving existing results is incompatible.

Preserve the original roster, Legend bonuses, established permanent reward amounts, diminishing speed returns and clutch rules when changing MVP selection or presentation.

**Why:** This work changes who earns the reward and makes it visible, not football balance or the reward system.

**How to apply:** Keep original Legend bonuses separate from earned MVP bonuses, show actual effective combined values, and retain historical slot-owned rewards without moving them to another player.
